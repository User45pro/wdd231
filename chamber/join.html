Membership Tiers & Benefits

Non-Profit Membership Benefits

We are proud to support community leaders and public service initiatives across Puebla.

  • Free directory listing in our member index
  • Access to monthly community roundtables
  • Volunteer matching with local business sponsors
  • Complimentary event registration for staff

Bronze Membership Benefits

Start connecting your commercial venture with local suppliers and customers.

  • Standard business directory listing with website link
  • Monthly newsletter business feature
  • Invitations to member networking mixers
  • 10% discount on business expo booth rentals

Silver Membership Benefits

Accelerate your growth with targeted advertising and professional development.

  • Enhanced directory listing with logo and company bio
  • Eligibility for homepage member spotlight rotation
  • 25% discount on Chamber expo booths and training workshops
  • Access to commercial legal advisory and compliance guides

Gold Membership Benefits

Maximum brand prestige, executive influence, and leadership opportunities.

  • Priority placement on directory and featured homepage spotlight
  • VIP seating at all annual conferences and galas
  • Full access to executive networking sessions with municipal leaders
  • Free expo booth at the annual Puebla Business Expo
